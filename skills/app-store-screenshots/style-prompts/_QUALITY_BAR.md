# Quality rules for optional styles

The customer brief and the design rules in `SKILL.md` take priority over every style sheet. Style sheets are historical references for explicit requests, not default recipes.

The [ASO playbook](../references/aso-screenshot-playbook.md), verified feature plan, and stage asset check-ins still apply when a style is selected. Ignore instructions to draw sample app UI, insert sample claims, or replace original Apple frames with a stylized device. Use the customer's real captures and local original frames. Do not propose download slides unless the user asks for them.

## Keep the customer visible

- Use the customer's actual app captures and approved brand material.
- Do not invent ratings, awards, testimonials, user counts, UI, or features.
- Use only the parts of a reference style that support the customer's content.
- Do not copy another app's mascot, content, or brand identity.

## Remove effects without a purpose

- Solid backgrounds and single-color headlines are valid.
- No required gradient, texture, badge, accent word, shadow stack, or decoration count.
- Do not add mascot volume, grain, or glow merely to pass a numeric rule.
- Repeated layouts and upright devices are valid. Connected mode is optional.
- Empty space can help the reader. Do not fill it to meet a density quota.

## Make the output readable

- Give each slide one clear benefit supported by its app capture.
- Keep important UI visible at thumbnail size.
- Use a conservative 4.5:1 headline contrast target; check every color behind text on non-solid backgrounds.
- Use type that belongs to the customer. Keep line breaks deliberate and prevent overflow.
- Check each language and device size separately.
- Never split text, faces, or important UI across a slide boundary.
- Inspect full-size PNGs and 160–220 pixel thumbnails before delivery.

A style is successful when the set communicates this customer's app. Decoration alone is not evidence of quality.
