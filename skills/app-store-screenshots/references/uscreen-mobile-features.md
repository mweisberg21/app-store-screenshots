# Uscreen mobile app capture guide

Source review: 24 September 2026. Read this for Uscreen customer work before selecting features. These are capture candidates, not a promise that every customer app includes every feature. Recheck the linked guide when a claim depends on a limit, platform, plan, or new release.

## Understand the customer app

Uscreen supplies native apps for branded content and membership services. The listing must explain the customer's content and member experience. It must not read like a sales page for Uscreen's admin tools. Keep the customer's name, voice, logo, photography, and content at the center. [Mobile and TV app overview](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/6708491-uscreen-mobile-tv-apps-overview)

Record the target app, released or intended release version, platform, capture date, and member access state. Ask which features are enabled and most valuable for this customer. Verify in the native app and supplied captures. Mark each candidate as **verified**, **unavailable**, or **needs evidence**. A website, generic Uscreen article, or another customer's app is not customer-specific proof.

## Match a benefit to a screen

Headline examples below are prompts for customer-specific copy. Use them only after verification. The suggested screenshots and asset requests are this team's capture advice.

| App area | Possible message and useful capture | What to verify or request |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Catalog, categories, search, filters | "Find your next class" for a class service. Show a populated catalog or useful filtered result. | Request the customer's featured categories and real thumbnails. Use the actual app's filter names; do not invent topics, counts, or filter controls. [Catalog guide](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/4315862-what-is-the-catalog) |
| Video or collection details | "Choose a guided program." Show the actual title, teacher, and lesson list where available. | Request the program or collection to feature and its approved cover. A single video page is not proof of a structured course, quiz, or certificate. [Program pages](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/4315940-program-pages-viewer-experience) |
| Playback | "Learn with your teacher." Show a clear moment from an approved video with enough app context to identify playback. | Request the video and preferred moment. Verify any caption, playback, or picture-in-picture claim on the target app; captions depend on content. Do not imply that all web player controls exist on mobile. [Program pages](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/4315940-program-pages-viewer-experience) |
| Continue Watching | "Pick up where you stopped." Show a real, partly watched item. | Use an approved demo account with watch history. A fresh account may not show the row. [Catalog guide](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/4315862-what-is-the-catalog) |
| Favorites and member playlists | "Keep your favorites together." Show a populated saved list or an actual member playlist. | Request a small content selection. Check the current navigation; these are different from admin-created catalog collections. The catalog guide distinguishes mobile playlists from TV support. [Catalog guide](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/4315862-what-is-the-catalog) |
| AirPlay or Chromecast | "Watch on your TV." Show the real casting control or connected state. | Verify a compatible receiver and successful playback. AirPlay is iOS-specific in this guide; Chromecast is documented for iOS and Android. Casting does not prove a dedicated TV app exists. [Mobile casting](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/4316140-mobile-apps-chromecast-airplay) |
| Live viewing and chat | "Join a live session." Show an actual member-facing event or live player. | Request an approved event and confirm access, timing, and chat availability. A recording is not a live session. Avoid stale event dates. [Live chat](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/4315896-live-chat) |
| Community | "Connect with your community." Show an approved channel or post in the member view. | Check enabled access and actual member controls. Obtain permission for visible names, faces, and posts, or use approved fictional demo accounts in the app. Direct messages and private channels need separate checks. [Community overview](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/5527044-community-overview) |
| Calendar and completion | "Plan your next session." Show scheduled content or real completion marks. | Ask for a useful week and confirm the Calendar is enabled. Mobile and web views differ. Completion marks show viewing activity; do not turn them into health, fitness, or learning results. [Calendar guide](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/7004269-calendar-complete-guide) |
| Community challenges | "Follow a daily challenge." Show a current challenge, its content, or real member progress where present. | Verify this customer's native challenge view, member access, dates, and actual progress behavior. Ask for its approved cover. The general guide includes web-specific behavior; do not infer mobile parity. [Challenges](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/8124897-community-challenges) |

Select the few areas that best explain the customer's value. Do not turn this table into a required ten-image deck. Creator photography and catalog artwork can support these screens, but cannot replace proof of the native app experience.

Lead with distinctive content, teachers, programs, and the member experience. Saved lists, casting, or completion are secondary candidates only when they add a strong customer benefit. Do not suggest downloads or offline viewing as a slide topic. If the user explicitly requests one, verify it in the target app and consult the [offline viewing guide](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/4315947-enable-download-for-offline-viewing). In-app offline viewing is not a permanent MP4 export.

## Important boundaries

- Navigation can be renamed and reordered. Use the customer's visible tab names in instructions and captions; do not assume a fixed "Browse → More" path. [Mobile tab customization](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/12530234-customize-mobile-tabs-titles-icons-order)
- Community and Calendar have plan and setup conditions in their guides. Check the actual customer configuration rather than treating a plan name as proof that a tab is enabled.
- Recommendations can depend on rollout, app version, and member history. The catalog and recommendations guides describe eligibility differently. Verify the target app before proposing an "AI" or personalization claim. [Catalog availability](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/4315862-what-is-the-catalog), [recommendations guide](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/14510041-video-recommendations)
- The mobile "Go Live" control is for admins. Do not advertise it as a member feature or start a live broadcast to obtain a screenshot. [Go Live from Mobile](https://help.uscreen.tv/en/articles/8358433-go-live-from-mobile)
- Confirm member access and paid content. A web offer is not proof of the same in-app offer. Do not invent a free trial, price, subscription entitlement, or purchase flow.
- Do not assume native iPad, Android, TV, or localized behavior from an iPhone capture. Get the matching capture for each requested device and language.

## Help the user obtain missing captures

At the feature-planning check-in, suggest a small capture list. For each item, give the purpose, route using the customer's tab names, member state, content to open, device, language, and capture needed. Example: "Open the approved program in the iPad app. Expand its lesson list. Capture the full screen in landscape. This will support the program slide."

Use an authorized demo account with fictional profile data. Capture the real app after content loads and transient alerts close. Save the original capture before layout work. Record the route and evidence in the brief. Do not use an admin dashboard, web page, drawn interface, or another customer's capture as a substitute.

If the agent has authorized device or simulator access, it can help navigate and capture the app. Otherwise, give the operator the steps and accept local files or attachments. Do not require credentials in chat. Do not publish posts, send messages, purchase content, or change customer settings merely to stage a screenshot.

If a required state cannot be captured, ask for another verified app area or keep the slide pending. Use drafts only for layout review and mark them clearly. Never export invented product evidence as final customer artwork.
